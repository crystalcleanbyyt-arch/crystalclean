Cleaning prices reflect the size, condition and work needed. Here is our Quick Home Refresh guide; other services are quoted individually.

Quick Home Refresh

Focused maintenance cleaning, generally around 2–3 hours. Pricing assumes normal upkeep, accessible surfaces and no heavy buildup.

Up to 2,000 sq ft$200Quick Home Refresh
2,001–2,500 sq ft$260Quick Home Refresh
2,501–3,000 sq ft$300Quick Home Refresh
Quick Refresh includes: essential kitchen cleaning, main living areas, basic bathroom cleaning, visible dusting, vacuuming and accessible floors. Not included: heavy limescale, deep buildup removal, extensive decluttering, or oven/refrigerator interiors. Exact scope is confirmed before booking.
